Blind, Deaf, and Mute

Legal information for the Minecraft Bedrock add-on and companion Discord bot that assign Blind, Deaf, and Mute roles to players.

Draft for operator review: Please verify these policies against the final bot implementation, especially data retention and deletion procedures, before submitting them for Discord application approval.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

How it works

Three participants join a shared Minecraft session. A separate Discord bot can apply server-level voice mute or deafen settings based on assigned roles, following session confirmation. Realm and local-world integration uses a manual code-and-confirmation process rather than automatic direct communication.

Contact

For general questions, use the repository issue tracker. Do not post private account details, tokens, or session codes in a public issue. For a private request, first ask the maintainer for a private contact method.
