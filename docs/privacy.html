Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026 · Operator: Daylightishere

Operator review required: The categories below reflect the planned bot architecture, not a completed audit. Verify actual data collection, logging, retention, hosting, and deletion before submitting this policy to Discord.

1. Scope

This policy concerns the Blind, Deaf, and Mute Discord bot and related Minecraft session coordination. Discord and Minecraft have their own privacy policies for their platforms.

2. Information the service may process

  • Discord account and server identifiers needed to identify linked participants, servers, and authorized session managers.
  • Minecraft player names or gamertags supplied for linking and session setup.
  • Session information, including role assignments, session identifiers, setup confirmations, and the state of bot-applied voice restrictions.
  • Technical diagnostics and operational logs needed for troubleshooting, reliability, and abuse prevention.

The intended service does not need to record voice audio or read the contents of private Discord messages. Confirm this against the deployed code and enabled bot intents.

3. How information is used

Information is used to link participants, coordinate role assignments, apply and restore voice permissions, recover sessions after bot restarts, diagnose errors, and protect the service against misuse.

4. Storage and retention

Session and voice-restriction records may be stored by the bot in its hosting environment to maintain sessions and recover after restarts. Records should be removed when they are no longer required for those purposes. Before publishing, the operator must verify and document the actual deletion triggers and log retention period.

5. Sharing

Information needed for operation is processed through Discord and the bot's hosting infrastructure. The service is not intended to sell personal data or use it for advertising. The operator must confirm any other processors or integrations before publication.

6. Security

Bot credentials and private setup codes should not be published. Access to hosted data should be restricted to authorized operators. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Access, correction, and deletion requests

You may request information about records associated with your participation or ask the operator to remove them, subject to technical and legal limitations. Start by opening a repository issue asking for a private contact method; do not post account IDs, session codes, or other private information publicly. The operator should verify ownership before acting on account-specific requests.

8. Changes

This policy may change as the bot develops or its hosting changes. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
