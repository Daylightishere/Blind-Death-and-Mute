1. Service

Blind, Deaf, and Mute is a community Minecraft Bedrock add-on and companion Discord bot. The add-on assigns gameplay roles; the Discord bot can apply server-level mute and deafen settings to participating members after a session manager confirms a matching setup receipt.

2. Agreement and eligibility

By using the bot or participating in a managed session, you agree to these terms and to applicable Discord, Minecraft, and server rules. You must meet the age and other eligibility requirements of the platforms you use.

3. Permissions and session managers

Server administrators are responsible for inviting the bot and granting only the permissions needed for its operation. Authorized session managers must obtain participants' agreement before starting sessions that alter voice permissions. The bot may server-mute or server-deafen participating members while a session is active. These restrictions can affect communication in a Discord voice channel.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use the service to harass others, bypass server moderation, impersonate users, misuse setup codes, or violate applicable law or platform rules. Do not publish private session codes, bot credentials, or other users' private information.

5. Limitations

Minecraft Realms and local multiplayer worlds cannot directly communicate with the separately hosted Discord bot using dedicated-server-only networking APIs. Manual session confirmation is therefore required. In-game Mute restrictions may not prevent every form of text communication. Features may be unavailable because of game updates, Discord permissions, connectivity, or platform limitations.
